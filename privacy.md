# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot processes information when it is used.

## 1. Purpose of the Bot

The bot is designed to assist with tracking and analyzing gameplay activity related to Dofus, including perceptor defense activity.

The bot is intended primarily for use within designated Discord servers and channels.

## 2. Information the Bot May Process

Depending on the features being used, the bot may process:

* Discord usernames and user IDs
* Discord message content in designated channels
* Images and screenshots uploaded to designated channels
* Information visible within submitted Dofus screenshots, such as character names and gameplay information
* Message metadata necessary for the bot to operate

The bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the bot is used only to provide its intended functionality, including:

* Reading submitted screenshots
* Extracting relevant Dofus gameplay information
* Identifying or validating character names
* Creating gameplay logs and records
* Allowing administrators or users to verify extracted information

## 4. Data Storage

Some submitted images and extracted gameplay information may be stored for gameplay tracking, verification, debugging, or record-keeping purposes.

Processing and storage may occur on computers or systems controlled by the bot administrator.

Data is not sold or used for advertising.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Information processed by the bot is not sold to third parties.

Information may be processed through Discord as necessary for the bot to function and through software operated by the bot administrator.

## 6. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with them where reasonably possible.

Requests can be made by contacting the bot administrator through the Discord server in which the bot is being used.

## 7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information. However, no computer system or online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the bot's features change.

Changes will be published on this page.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the bot administrator through the Discord server in which the bot is being used.
